# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Eleventy on GitHub Pages, confirmed by the owner. Profile copy is maintained
in the repository and published as static HTML.

## Users

Engineering peers looking to understand what Arjay builds, how he learns, and
which public repositories are worth exploring.

## Product Purpose

Host Arjay Sitoy’s profile, eventually describe what he does at work, and
give him a place to publish ideas and personal writing. The first release
answers who he is and shows concrete examples of his public engineering work.

## Evidence on Hand

The owner's public GitHub profile identifies him as a software engineer based
in the Philippines. His public lab repositories document infrastructure,
developer tooling, and experiments with local AI. They do not establish an
employer, job history, client work, or production outcomes.

## Product Principles

- Link to actual work rather than substituting skill lists or metrics.
- Distinguish personal experiments from employment.
- Keep the profile readable without client-side JavaScript.
- Add work pages and writing when there is something to publish, not before.
